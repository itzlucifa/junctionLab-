Why these timings?

Both clearance intervals come from the physics of the scene, not from a rule of thumb. Every input is a named constant at the top of the core, so overriding one changes the derived values everywhere.

Emergency vehicle

With the corridor on, signals clear the ambulance's arm and queued vehicles make way. Switch it off to see the delay it normally causes.

How waiting is measured

A vehicle is "waiting" while it moves slower than 8 px/s before the stop line, including time spent queued beyond the screen edge. Average wait counts vehicles that have crossed and those still waiting.
